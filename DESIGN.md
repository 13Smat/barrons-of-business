# Introduction
Rts game where the FTC Announces that "all is fair in love, war, and now TRADE!" Gloves off when it comes to retail. Most of all in the retail space where the companies are sabotaging each other in a fight to the bottom of morals. PVP or PVE against other retailers. Secure factories and resources to further expand your verticals and horizontal monopolies. Crush the competition at all costs by hiring scammers, sabotage production supply lines, and shut down combatant stores in various violent and non violent ways. Welcome to Baron's of Business

## Key mechanics:
### Resources:
1. build materials (B)
2. food (F)
3. money ($)
4. production materials - base materials have 3 forms - Ore(raw), Plates(refined, usable for crafting), Bar (efficient storage)

### Game Start:
Pick a retailer to be the CEO of:
  1. General retailer: -Jack of all trades, Large Store Footprint
  2. Grocery Store: -Lots of (F), little (B), moderate ($), Medium Store Footprint
  3. Home Improvement: -Little (F), Lots of (B), moderate ($), Large Store Footprint, early access to mining equipment
  4. Bank: -Very little (F), Very little (B), High ($), tier 1 defenses built in, Small Store Footprint

Pick a color scheme from pre selected list:
  - All entities with a color parameter will have the styling based on the color scheme

### Player Views:
  1. Google Earth 3D building like view. Birds eye with a cutout of the building outer walls to see the Retails space. Camera can rotate around the property but will always have a "home" orientation that is set so the retail space is left or at the right
  2. Underground view - Switches to a 3d underground map with symbols for different entities in the tunnels

### Game Objective:
  1. Be the preferred retailer by winning 70% of market share
